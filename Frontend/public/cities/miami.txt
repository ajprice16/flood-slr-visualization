Miami is highly vulnerable to sea level rise due to its low elevation and porous limestone bedrock. Even moderate flooding could impact millions of residents.

Beyond those whose homes will be completely underwater, many more will have their foundations destabilized and experience flooding far more often as the ocean works to reshape the coastline.

"The internal colony may be powerless to resist or, paralleling the development model, may decide to accept such hazards to generate revenue. Whether chosen or not, once lands are degraded, additional damage is likely to follow as the cycle of exploitation continues." — Mountains of Injustice

The rising waters will do the opposite of empowerment and further trap people in the cycle of exploitation.
